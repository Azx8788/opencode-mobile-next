# First-hour accessibility and background audit

Finish line: identify source-backed failures and the recovery already available for notification denial, Android background limits, tablet input, enlarged text, Arabic, and long output. Non-goal: claiming a fresh device qualification or editing another owner's widget, OC1, or agent installation files.

Evidence is source inspection on `codex/edge-sweep` and the named historical QA records. This audit launched no tests, emulator, native build, or device action. Regression execution belongs to the coordinating agent.
